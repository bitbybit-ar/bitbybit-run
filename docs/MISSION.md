# 🌱 Mission — Bit by Bit Run

**A 60-second race that leaves you a little healthier, a little smarter about
money, and a lot harder to scam — one bit at a time.**

## Why we built this

Most "educational" apps lecture. People bounce. Games don't lecture — they
reward, they repeat, they stick. So we hid real lessons inside a runner race you
actually want to play again: every sprint, every dodged trap, every tip to the
winner teaches something without ever feeling like a lesson.

Bit by Bit Run is fun first. The learning rides along for free.

## What we believe

- **Learning should feel like playing.** If it's fun, people come back — and
  repetition is how habits and instincts form.
- **Consequences teach better than warnings.** Eat junk and get sent back to the
  start; you _feel_ the lesson instead of reading it.
- **The best lessons are the ones you discover yourself.** We set the stage; the
  player draws the conclusion.
- **Good tools belong to everyone.** Free to play, open, and running on free,
  public infrastructure — no paywall, no gatekeeping.

## The pillars

Each one maps to something you actually do in the game.

### 1. 🏃 Move well, eat well

The race runs on a risk/reward food loop. Grab good food (**⚡**) and you sprint;
swallow junk (**🍔**) and the poison bar fills until it sends you back to the
start. It's the real world in miniature: **good fuel powers you forward, junk
drags you down** — and here the feedback is instant and visceral. The game turns
"eat better" from a nag into a reflex you build while having fun.

### 2. ⚡ Bitcoin & Lightning, hands-on

You don't read about self-custody here — you _use_ it. You sign in with your own
**Nostr identity**, and the winner of a race can be tipped over **Lightning**
with a real **zap**. Value moves **peer-to-peer**, with no middleman taking a
cut. And the social default we celebrate is **generosity** — zap the winner —
rather than speculation.

### 3. 🎣 Spot the scam (the fake ads, as a plus)

The web's crypto corners are full of "You won 1 BTC!" and "double your sats"
bait. So we built a **scam museum**: deliberately fake ad banners and a parody
"watch an ad to keep playing" interstitial, where falling for one costs you
nothing but earns you a punchline and a lesson. Players leave with the single
most useful money skill there is — **smelling a scam from across the room** —
practiced somewhere completely safe. _If it looks too good to be true, it's a
scam._

### 4. 🌍 Open and accessible to all

Anyone can play — there's a **free demo with no login**, it runs great on a
**phone**, and it speaks **English and Spanish**. Under the hood it costs **$0 to
run**: serverless, riding free public relays and peer-to-peer Lightning. Access
shouldn't depend on your wallet or your hardware.

### 5. 🤝 Play together

Races are **multiplayer** — up to four runners, live, on the same track. The
energy is friendly competition and a community that cheers (and tips) the winner,
because learning sticks better when you're doing it with other people.

## Who it's for

- **The Bitcoin-curious** who want a friendly, hands-on first taste of Nostr and
  Lightning.
- **Anyone who wants a nudge** toward healthier, more deliberate choices — served
  as play, not as a guilt trip.
- **Everyone who's ever _almost_ clicked** a "free Bitcoin" banner.

## Our north star

A world where understanding your money and taking care of your body are things
you pick up while genuinely enjoying yourself — bit by bit, race by race.
